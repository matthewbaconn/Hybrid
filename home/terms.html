Health and safety

General information only

Mileset gives general fitness and nutrition information for healthy adults. It is not medical advice and doesn't replace a doctor, registered dietitian or other health professional. Mileset is not a dietitian, nutritionist or nutrition counselor.

Check with a doctor first if you

  • are pregnant or breastfeeding
  • have a heart, lung, kidney, liver or blood sugar condition, high blood pressure, or any other medical condition
  • take medication that affects heart rate, blood sugar or appetite
  • are coming back from an injury, surgery or illness
  • haven't exercised in a long time

Don't use Mileset's calorie targets if you

  • are under 18
  • have, or have had, an eating disorder
  • are being treated by a doctor for a condition that needs a special diet (for example diabetes or kidney disease)

Stop and get help

Stop exercising and get medical help right away if you have chest pain or pressure, fainting, severe dizziness, trouble breathing, an irregular heartbeat, or sharp or unusual pain. In an emergency, call 911.

Numbers are estimates

Calorie, macro, pace, heart rate zone, fitness and race time numbers are estimates and can be off. Food read from photos and text, coach notes and workout reviews are written by AI and can be wrong. Check food labels when you can, and listen to your body over the app.

Built-in safety limits

  • A weight-loss calorie target never goes below 1,500 calories a day (or your maintenance, if that's lower).
  • The deficit is capped at 750 calories, and shrinks if you lose more than about 1% of your body weight in a week.
  • Every 8th week is a maintenance week, and targets go to maintenance once you reach your goal.
  • Goal weights more than 20% below your current weight aren't accepted.

If food or weight feels stressful

Talking to someone helps. The National Alliance for Eating Disorders helpline is free and staffed by licensed therapists: 866-662-1235 (Monday to Friday, 9 am to 7 pm ET).
