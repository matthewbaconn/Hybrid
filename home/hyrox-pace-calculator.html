The race format

A HYROX race is eight 1 km runs, each followed by a station: SkiErg 1,000 m, Sled Push 50 m, Sled Pull 50 m, Burpee Broad Jumps 80 m, Rowing 1,000 m, Farmers Carry 200 m, Sandbag Lunges 100 m, then Wall Balls. Weights and wall ball reps depend on your division, so check your division's official rules before you set station targets.

Where the time goes

Running is about half the race, so your run pace moves your finish the most. An analysis of over 460,000 individual results found that in the Open divisions, running made up about 47% of the time gap between the top 15% and the middle of the field, and the eight stations about 42%. The stations with the biggest proportional gaps were Burpee Broad Jumps, Wall Balls, Sled Pull and Sandbag Lunges.

Consistency matters too. In a small look at London 2022 Pro results, podium athletes had a median gap of 17 seconds between their fastest and slowest of runs 2 to 7; the comparison group's gap was about twice that, and they spent more time in the Roxzone. The lesson: pick a run pace you can hold after the sleds, and walk into stations with a plan instead of standing around.

How to use the card

Run 1 always feels easy. Hold your planned pace there, because going out fast costs you on the sleds and lunges. If you're behind the clock after the sled pull, don't chase it on run 4; most of the race is still ahead.
