Train together

The first four weeks decide it. Don't do them alone.

Pick one friend you run or lift with. You each get your own plan, built around your own days. You start the same week and keep each other honest.

Why the first four weeks

A 2026 study followed 389,481 people logging strength workouts in a training app for a year. How consistently someone trained in their first 28 days was the strongest predictor of whether they were still training months later. Only about 1 in 10 beginners was still training weekly at the one-year mark.

Source: Predictors of long-term resistance exercise adherence, Frontiers in Sports and Active Living, Aug 2026. An observational study of one app's users; it shows a link, not a guarantee.

How to start together

  1. Send your partner the invite belowText it, or drop it in the group chat you already train in.
  2. Both build a planYour goal and the days you can train: 5K to marathon, triathlon, HYROX, strength. Running and lifting sit in the same week.
  3. Do day one the same dayThen a quick check-in each morning. On a bad night of sleep, your plan eases off.
  4. Compare notes once a weekFour weeks, then decide if it's working for you.
