Your plan

A real plan, built for your week.

Tell Mileset your goal, how fit you are now and which days you can train. You get every session laid out, from easy days and long days to strength and race week.

  • Search races anywhere in the world and your plan counts down to it
  • Strength days that don't wreck your runs
  • Paces and targets from your own fitness
Recovery

Tired? Your plan already knows.

A 20-second morning check-in plus your watch's sleep, resting heart rate and HRV give you a readiness score. On low days, the workout gets shorter or easier.

  • Readiness, sleep and soreness at a glance
  • Hard sessions move when you need a lighter day
Food

Food targets that move with your training.

Your calories start from your height, weight, age and how active your day is, then add what you burn in workouts. Snap a photo or scan a barcode to log a meal.

  • Lose, maintain or gain, at a safe pace
  • Race fueling plans and meal prep with a grocery list
