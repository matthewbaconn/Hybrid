How to use your race pace

Even pacing, or running the second half slightly faster, is how most distance records and good races are run. Going out fast costs more than it saves, because the early surge burns through carbohydrate you need later. Practice goal pace in training: a few miles of it inside a long run tells you far more than any calculator.

Why the marathon prediction can be too fast

Riegel's formula assumes you're as trained for the long race as the short one. A study of recreational runners found it is accurate up to the half marathon, but its marathon predictions were at least 10 minutes too fast for half of the runners. Adding weekly mileage made the predictions better, because the marathon rewards volume. If you lift a lot and run less, or the marathon is your first, add a cushion to the predicted time and let your long runs decide.
