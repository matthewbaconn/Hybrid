Founding Ambassadors

We're building Mileset with a small group of athletes from the start. Ambassadors get free access for life and a spot on this website. In return, we ask for a little of your time and honest feedback.

What you get

  • Lifetime free access to Mileset, kept even if you only use the plan for four weeks
  • Official ambassador recognition
  • A featured profile on this website with your name and story
  • Early access to new features
  • Your own invite link, with a page showing who joins through you

What we ask

  • Post or share Mileset 2-3 times a week. Stories count, including a story that shares your Mileset signup link.
  • Use your Mileset plan for at least 4 weeks.

These are the terms of the ambassador program. Your free access stays with you either way.
