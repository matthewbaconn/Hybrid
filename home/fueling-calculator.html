    How the numbers work

    Under about 45 minutes you don't need carbs. For harder efforts of 45–75 minutes, small amounts (even a sports-drink mouth rinse) can help. From roughly 1 to 2.5 hours, 30–60 g an hour is the usual target. Past 2.5 hours, up to 90 g an hour can help, but only with glucose-fructose blends (most gels that list both), because a single sugar is absorbed at about 60 g an hour at most. Easy, low-intensity runs burn less carbohydrate, so this tool aims lower for them. Build up to higher amounts over a few weeks of long runs: guts adapt.
